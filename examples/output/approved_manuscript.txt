The Extra Chair

I set a cup beside my own.
Then I pulled out the little blue chair.
"Who's coming?" asked Dad.
"I haven't decided yet."

I invited the wooden duck.
He tipped into his cup, beak first.
I invited my longest sock.
It couldn't sit up.

The room was very quiet.
Dad stood in the doorway with two biscuits.
"Is that seat taken?"
I looked at the duck. I looked at the sock.

"They can share," I said.
Dad squeezed into the little blue chair.
His knees came up beside his ears.
I got another plate.
